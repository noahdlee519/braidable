What I do

Find

I sit down with your managers and the people doing the work, map where time goes, and sort what AI can automate today from what it can't. You get a short, ranked list with what each change is worth and what it would take.

Build

Where something can be built, I build it: automations for repetitive work, assistants that know your documents, and tools wired into the software your team already uses. Where an off-the-shelf product does the job, I'll say so.

Teach

Tools only help if people use them. I run hands-on sessions with your teams, write plain guides for the tools we build, and show people how to use AI well and safely in their own work.

How a project runs

  1. Talk

  2. Map

  3. Build

  4. Teach

    Training for your teams, and support while the new way of working settles in.
