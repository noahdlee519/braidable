What I do

Build

Custom AI tools, from a small internal helper to a product your customers use: assistants that know your documents, automations that take over repetitive work, and prototypes you can put in front of people within weeks.

Strategy

A clear plan for where AI helps your business and where it doesn't. We look at your work, your data, and your budget, and you leave with a short list of projects ranked by what they're worth.

Consulting

A second pair of eyes for your team: reviewing an AI project already under way, choosing between models and vendors, or training people to use the tools well and safely.

How a project runs

  1. Talk

    A free 30-minute call about what you want to change and what's in the way.

  2. Plan

    A written proposal with the scope, the timeline, and a fixed price.

  3. Build

    Short rounds with something working to try at the end of each.

  4. Hand over

    Documentation, training for your team, and support while it settles in.
